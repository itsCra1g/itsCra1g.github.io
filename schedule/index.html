Tips

  • Post it at the same time every week, like Sunday evening or Monday morning, so people know where to look.
  • Pin it in Discord and on your X profile, and put it in your Twitch and Kick schedule settings too, so viewers get reminders.
  • Only promise what you'll do. A shorter schedule you stick to builds more trust than a full one you don't.
  • A second time zone helps viewers in other countries; pick where most of your chat is.
  • Need panels to match? Use the same colour and font in the Panel Maker.
