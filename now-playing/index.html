Make your Spotify app

Spotify lets each app connect only a handful of accounts, so everyone makes their own. It's free and takes about two minutes, and you only do it once.

  1. Open the Spotify Developer Dashboard and log in with the Spotify account you stream music from.
  2. Press Create app. Call it anything (say, My Now Playing) and add any short description.
  3. Under Redirect URIs, paste this address exactly and press Add:
  1. Under Which API/SDKs are you planning to use?, tick Web API. Agree to the terms and press Save.
  2. On the app's page, copy the Client ID. You'll paste it in step 2.
Spotify Premium needed. Spotify only lets Premium accounts own a developer app. That's their rule, not ours.
2

Connect Spotify

Paste your Client ID and press Connect. Spotify asks you to allow access, and the overlay can only see what's playing. It can't control your music or see anything else.

Add it to OBS

Connect Spotify in step 2 and your OBS link appears here.

This is your overlay link, with your style built in:

Keep this link private. It's hidden here in case you're live. Anyone with it could see what you're listening to, so don't paste it in chat or show it on stream.
  1. In OBS, press + under Sources and pick Browser.
  2. Paste the link into URL.
  3. Set the size to 680 × 220. Resize it on your scene as you like and it keeps its shape.
  4. Press OK and play a song.

Changed the style? Copy the new link and paste it over the old one in the browser source.

If something's not right

What you seeWhat to do
Spotify says INVALID_CLIENT: Invalid redirect URIThe redirect URI in your Spotify app doesn't match. Copy it again from step 1, add it in the app's settings and save.
Spotify says the user isn't registeredYou logged in with a different Spotify account from the one that owns the app. Log in with the owner, or add the other account under User Management in the app.
Nothing shows in OBSPlay a song: it stays hidden while nothing is playing, and while paused if you ticked that. Check the browser source size matches step 4.
It says lost its Spotify linkCome back here, connect again (it's one click), and paste the new link into your browser source.
The glow doesn't match the album artSome covers won't share their colours with the browser. It falls back to Pilot blue for those.
