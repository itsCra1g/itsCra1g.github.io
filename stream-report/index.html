A stream report in your Discord, every time.

When you go offline, Streamer.bot posts how the stream went: how long, peak and average viewers, new followers and subs, gifts, bits and Kicks, chat and raids. Twitch and Kick counted together.

Make a Discord webhook

A webhook is a link that lets Streamer.bot post into one of your channels, like #stream-reports. You need to be able to manage the server.

  1. In Discord, open Server Settings → Integrations → Webhooks and press New Webhook.
  2. Give it a name and picture (that's who the post comes from), and pick the channel to post in.
  3. Press Copy Webhook URL and paste it below.
Keep the webhook private. Anyone with it can post in that channel. This page doesn't store it or send it anywhere except Discord when you press Send test post.

If something's not right

    

      
What you seeWhat to do

      
The test post failsCopy the webhook URL again from Discord. If the webhook was deleted, make a new one.

      
Nothing posts after a streamCheck the action is enabled with its Stream Online and Stream Offline triggers, that Streamer.bot was running when you went live, and that the stream was longer than your minimum.

      
Viewers are missingAdd the Viewer Count Update triggers. They update every few minutes, so very short streams may not have any.

      
A bot is the top chatterAdd its name, in lower case, to the Bots list near the bottom of the code.
