Putting them on your channel

Twitch

  1. Go to your channel page and open the About section.
  2. Turn on Edit Panels, then press the big + to add a panel.
  3. Upload the image, paste the text into the description, and add an image link if you want the picture to be clickable (for example your Discord invite).
  4. Press Submit, and repeat in the order you want them.

Kick

  1. Go to your channel's About tab and choose Edit Panels.
  2. Add a panel, upload the image and paste the text. Panels are the same 320 px width as Twitch, so the same files work.

YouTube has no panels. Put the same text in your channel description and use the Links field for your socials.

4

Tips

  • Five to eight panels is plenty. About, Schedule, Rules and Socials cover most channels; add Discord, Setup, Support or Sub perks if you have them.
  • Keep them current. An out-of-date schedule is worse than none. If you can't keep a panel up to date, leave it out.
  • Put the important stuff first: people rarely scroll past the first row.
  • Keep links in the text short and use the image link for the main one.

Stay private. Don't put your full name, town, workplace or school in panels, and never link a wishlist that ships to your home address. Use a separate email for your channel.
