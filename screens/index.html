Add them to OBS

One link per screen, with everything above built in:

Starting Soon
Be Right Back
Stream Ending
  1. In each scene (Starting Soon, BRB, Ending), press + under Sources and pick Browser.
  2. Paste that screen's link into URL and set the size to 1920 × 1080.
  3. Tick Refresh browser when scene becomes active. That's what restarts a "from when the scene appears" timer each time you switch to it.
  4. Press OK. Using Transparent? Put your own background below it in the scene.

Changed something? Copy the new link and paste it over the old one. This page remembers your settings on this browser.

If something's not right

What you seeWhat to do
The countdown doesn't restart when I switch scenesTick Refresh browser when scene becomes active in the browser source's settings.
The clock-time countdown is outIt uses your PC's clock and time zone, so check those are right.
It's cut off or has bordersSet the browser source to exactly 1920 × 1080 and let it fill the canvas.
The animation stuttersSwitch to Transparent with your own background, which is lighter on OBS.
