A webcam frame that reacts to your chat.

A glowing border around your camera that pulses for follows, lights up for subs and gifts, and goes big for raids and gift bombs, on Twitch and Kick together. Add your name to the bottom and it flashes up who just supported you.

1

Turn on Streamer.bot's WebSocket server

The frame reacts to events straight from Streamer.bot (1.0 or later), connected to Twitch, Kick or both. There's no action or code to add.

  1. In Streamer.bot, open Servers/Clients → WebSocket Server.
  2. Tick Auto Start and press Start Server. Leave it on 127.0.0.1 port 8080 unless you've changed it.

Already set up for Alerts, Activity Feed or Combined Chat? Then you're done.

Pick what it reacts to

Reaction colour

Follows get a quick pulse; subs, resubs, gifts, cheers and Kicks a bigger double pulse; raids and gift bombs a big burst. Untick any you don't want. The wording is what the name tag shows: use {name}, {amount} and {months}.

4

Add it to OBS

Your frame link, with your design built in:

  1. In OBS, press + under Sources and pick Browser.
  2. Paste the link into URL. Set the size to match your camera's shape: 1280 × 720 for a normal 16:9 webcam, or the same width and height for a circle.
  3. Press OK, put the frame above your camera in the Sources list, then drag and resize it so the border sits around your camera. The frame is drawn a little in from the edges so the glow has room.

Changed the design? Copy the new link and paste it over the old one.
